# cueCode Landing Page

## Description 

cueCode is a personal website that functions as a professional portfolio. Its purpose is to present who I am, what I do, and what I'm currently working on. Through this page, I share information about my academic background, my interests, my projects in progress, and a direct contact channel.
The site is designed as a digital cover letter to present my profile in a clear, organized, and visually appealing way. It also includes access to my resume and links to my social media and contact information.
